S EC TION 1

Purpose and Scope

This document sets out the standards of service the client receives. OH-01 Order Handbook governs
how the work is carried out. This document governs what the client experiences while it is being
carried out.

1.1        These standards apply to every order without exception.

1.2        Where a standard cannot be met, the client is informed before the standard is missed rather
           than afterwards.

1.3        Standards are measured and reported weekly.


How performance is assessed
The profile is assessed through two channels. Only one of them is visible.
C H A N N EL                                   VISIBLE        A S S ES S M EN T W EI G H T


Public review and star rating                     Yes         Contributes to the displayed rating

Private questionnaire                             No          Carries significant weight in the Success
                                                              Score


1.1        The private questionnaire is issued to the client 24 hours after the order concludes and
           remains open for 60 days.

1.2        It covers the quality of the work, whether the work met the client's expectations, and
           whether the work was useful.

1.3        Responses are not visible to the seller and cannot be identified, addressed or corrected.

1.4        First time buyers and higher value orders carry greater weight.

1.5        Clients are not asked to provide public reviews or private feedback at any stage.
           Performance in this channel is influenced only by the standard of service delivered during the order.


   T H E C O N S EQ U EN C E

   The only period during which the client's assessment can be influenced is while the order is open.
   Once the order concludes, the assessment is submitted privately and cannot be recovered. This is
   why the standards in Sections 3 and 4 are treated as mandatory rather than advisory.

Stages of an Order

The client passes through six stages. At each stage there is a specific concern to be addressed.
S TA G E                                    T H E C L I EN T ' S P R I N C I PA L C O N C ER N


1. First contact                            Whether anyone is available, and whether their message has
                                            been read

2. Considering the purchase                 Whether the price is justified and what happens if the work is not
                                            right

3. Order placed                             What happens next, and whether they explained themselves
                                            adequately

4. Initial draft                            Whether the work reflects what they asked for

5. Revisions                                Whether the work is moving toward what they wanted

6. Delivery                                 Whether everything is present and whether the files will work

S EC TION 3

Communication Standards

3.1        A first response to a new inquiry is issued within 10 minutes at any hour.

3.2        A response to a message on an active order is issued within 30 minutes during covered
           hours.

3.3        A revision request is acknowledged within 15 minutes.

3.4        Where a client's tone becomes adverse, the Head of Customer Success is informed within
           15 minutes.

3.5        The team member on shift responds. Responses are not deferred to a particular role.

3.6        A message received outside covered hours is the first action of the following shift.

3.7        The client is not asked to repeat information already provided.

3.8        A team member taking over an order does not introduce themselves as new to it.


   A S S ES S M EN T O F C O M M U N I C AT I O N

   Fiverr assesses effective communication on five components: availability, the clarity of expectations
   set at the outset, explanation of the process, progress shared during the order, and issues addressed
   as they arise. Speed of response is one of five. The remaining four are governed by Section 4.

S EC TION 4

Order Standards

4.1        The brief is confirmed with the client within 15 minutes of the order starting.

4.2        The contents of the final delivery are stated to the client before design work begins.

4.3        Where the package purchased does not include vector or source files, the client is informed
           at the outset.

4.4        The initial draft is shared within 48 hours and serves as the direction check.

4.5        The initial draft is accompanied by an explanation of the direction taken.

4.6        The client is asked what they would change, rather than whether they are satisfied.

4.7        All feedback is collected before revisions are assigned.

4.8        Changes are restated to the client before a set of revisions is assigned.

4.9        Work outside the purchased package is quoted at the time it is requested.

4.10       Where a delivery date is at risk, the client is informed before the date rather than after it.


   THE INITIAL D RAFT

   The initial draft is the principal point at which the direction of the work can be corrected at low cost,
   and the principal opportunity to influence the client's eventual assessment. It is shared in the order
   conversation. Final delivery is made separately on the order page.

S EC TION 5

Delivery Standards

5.1        Final delivery is made on the order page. Draft work is not submitted through the delivery
           function.

5.2        Every file is verified against the delivery contents stated before work began.

5.3        Files are checked for watermarks and correct formats, and the fonts used are named.

5.4        Delivery is made ahead of the due date wherever the work allows.

5.5        The delivery message states what the folder contains.

5.6        Where a client identifies an omission falling within the stated contents, it is supplied without
           dispute.

Where a client wishes to withdraw

6.1        Two options are offered. A senior designer produces a fresh direction at no additional cost
           against a stated date, or a partial refund is issued and the order concluded.

6.2        Where the first option is offered, the designer and the date are stated. General assurances
           are not given.

6.3        Both options require the approval of the Head of Customer Success.

6.4        The decision and its reason are recorded.


   M EA S U R ES T H AT D O N O T A F F EC T T H E S U C C ES S S C O R E

   Fiverr confirms that the following are not penalised: clients who cease responding, revisions, delivery
   extensions, partial refunds, and cancellations arising from a misunderstanding or an order placed in
   error. Reluctance to use these measures leads to disagreement, which is assessed under conflict free
   orders.
