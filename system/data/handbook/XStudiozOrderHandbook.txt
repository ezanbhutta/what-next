S EC TION 1

Purpose and Scope

This document defines the standard process for handling an order on the XStudioz profile. It applies
to every order without exception, including controlled orders.

1.1        The process is identical for all orders. No distinction is made in the treatment of any client.

1.2        Fiverr collects order requirements through a form that cannot be amended. The form does
           not gather sufficient information, so the working brief is established by the team in the order
           chat.

1.3        The purchased package defines what is delivered. Anything beyond it is quoted as additional
           work.

1.4        Order status, notes and internal records are maintained in ClickUp. Client communication
           takes place on Fiverr.


Process summary
S TA G E                                         S TA N D A R D


1. Brief confirmed                               Within 15 minutes of the order starting

2. Delivery scope stated                         Before design work begins

3. Brief issued to production                    Same day

4. Initial draft shared                          Within 48 hours

6. Revisions assigned                            As a single set

7. Final delivery                                On the order page, ahead of the due date

S EC TION 3

Confirming the Brief

No design work begins until the brief has been confirmed by the client in writing.

3.1       The brief is confirmed within 15 minutes of the order starting.

3.2       Where the client has already provided information, it is restated for their approval rather than
          requested again.
          The client is asked to confirm or correct. This is faster for the client and produces a written record.

3.3       Where information has not been provided, it is requested in a single message rather than
          across several.

3.4       The client's written confirmation constitutes the agreed brief for the duration of the order.

3.5       The confirmed brief is recorded in ClickUp on the same day.

S EC TION 4

Delivery Scope

The client is told what the delivery will contain before design work begins. This is stated by the team
and does not require the client to specify anything.

4.1        The contents of the final delivery are stated to the client before design work begins.

4.2        The statement lists the file formats and assets included in the package purchased.

4.3        Where the package purchased does not include vector or source files, this is stated at the
           outset.
           It is not left to be discovered at delivery.

4.4        Anything the client requires beyond the stated contents is quoted as additional work at the
           time.

4.5        The stated contents form the checklist against which the delivery is verified.

S EC TION 5

Design and Initial Draft

5.1          The confirmed brief is issued to production in ClickUp on the day it is agreed.

5.2          The initial draft is shared with the client within 48 hours and serves as the direction
             check.
             It is the point at which the client confirms the work is proceeding in the direction they intended.


5.3          The initial draft is accompanied by an explanation of the direction taken, referring to the
             confirmed brief.

5.4          The client is asked what they would change rather than whether they are satisfied.
             A general question invites a general answer and defers the substantive feedback to a later stage.


5.5          The client is asked to provide all feedback together rather than in separate messages.



      W H Y T H E I N I T I A L D R A F T M AT T ER S

      The initial draft is the only stage at which the direction of the work can be corrected at low
      cost. It is also the principal opportunity to influence the private feedback the client will later
      provide, since that feedback is submitted after the order closes and cannot be seen or
      addressed.

S EC TION 6

Revisions

The gig provides unlimited revisions. The number of revisions requested is not restricted. This
section governs how revisions are processed.

6.1        All feedback is collected before revisions are assigned to production.

6.2        Revisions are assigned as a single set. Individual changes are not assigned separately.

6.3        The client is asked at the initial draft stage to provide all feedback together.
           Requests arriving individually extend the order without adding value for the client.

6.4        Before a set of revisions is assigned, the changes are restated to the client for confirmation.

6.5        Where a request falls outside the purchased package it is quoted. It is not absorbed.

6.6        Where an order reaches a fourth round of revisions the Head of Customer Success is
           informed.
           This is a record, not an approval gate. A fourth round indicates a shortcoming earlier in the process.


   R ED U C I N G R EV I S I O N S

   Revisions cannot be limited by contract. They are reduced by confirming the brief properly, stating the
   delivery contents in advance, and using the initial draft to establish direction before the work is
   refined.

S EC TION 7

Final Delivery

7.1       The final delivery is made on the order page. Draft work is shared in chat and is not
          submitted through the delivery function.

7.2       Every file is verified against the delivery contents stated to the client before work began.

7.3       Files are checked for watermarks, correct formats and the fonts used, which are named in
          the delivery.

7.4       Delivery is made ahead of the due date wherever the work allows.

7.5       The delivery message states what the folder contains.

7.6       Where a client identifies something missing after delivery, it is supplied without dispute if it
          falls within the stated contents.

S EC TION 8

Shift Continuity

Orders run across shifts. The client should not be able to identify the point at which one shift
ended and another began.

8.1           The last ten messages on an order are read before any reply is sent.

8.2           A team member taking over an order does not introduce themselves as new to it. The
              conversation continues.

8.3           Information already provided by the client is not requested again.

8.4           The handover records the order reference, its current stage, the client's most recent
              request, the next action and any point requiring care.

8.5           The confirmed brief and every material order event are recorded in ClickUp, not held in
              the Fiverr conversation alone.



      C O N T I N U I T Y I S A S S ES S ED

      Approximately one client in five identifies that they are dealing with more than one person. This
      is assessed under effective communication, which is the area currently holding the profile
      back.
