S EC TION 1

Purpose and Scope

This document governs the handling of inquiries from first contact until an order is placed or the
inquiry is closed. Once an order exists, OH-01 Order Handbook applies.

1.1        Follow up applies to inquiries only. Clients holding an active order are not followed up; their
           order is progressed under OH-01.

1.2        Message content and phrasing are already established and are not governed by this
           document. This document sets the standards, not the wording.

1.3        Every inquiry is recorded in the inquiry log, including the price quoted and the date of each
           follow up.

S EC TION 2

Response Standards

2.1          A first response is issued within 10 minutes at any hour.

2.2          The team member on shift responds. Response is not restricted by role.

2.3          No price is provided before the requirements are understood.

2.4          Messages identified as spam are reported as spam within 24 hours.

2.5          No shift ends with an inquiry unanswered.

S EC TION 3

Qualification

No quotation is issued before qualification is complete.

3.1          Four points are established before a price is provided: budget range, deadline, who
             provides approval, and what the client requires.

3.2          Where a budget range is discussed, a range is offered rather than requested. This allows
             the client to confirm or decline without negotiation.

3.3          Qualification is recorded in the inquiry log at the time it is obtained.

3.4          An inquiry handed from one shift to another carries all four points. An inquiry handed over
             incomplete is returned.



      P U R P O S E O F Q U A L I F I C AT I O N

      An order sold without a clear understanding of the requirement cannot be recovered later in
      production. The quality of the handover from inquiry to order determines the number of
      revisions that follow.

S EC TION 4

Quotation

4.1        Quotations are drawn from the published package structure. Prices are not constructed
           for individual inquiries.

4.2        A package above the level indicated by the client is presented alongside the level
           requested, with the difference expressed in deliverables.

4.3        The price quoted is recorded in the inquiry log at the time it is sent.

4.4        Quotations above USD 500 require the approval of the Head of Customer Success before
           they are issued.

4.5        Where a client indicates the price is beyond their budget, the scope is reduced.
           The price is not reduced for unchanged scope.


4.6        No commitment to a delivery date is given before designer availability has been
           confirmed.

S EC TION 5

Follow Up and Closure

5.1        Every inquiry receives three follow ups before it is closed.

5.2        Follow ups are issued on day one, day three and day seven from the date the quotation
           was sent.

5.3        Each follow up addresses a different point. A quotation is not simply restated.

5.4        The date of each follow up is recorded in the inquiry log.

5.5        No fourth follow up is issued.

5.6        An inquiry is closed only after three recorded follow ups, with a reason recorded.

5.7        An inquiry closed without three recorded follow ups is reopened and reassigned.


Declining an inquiry

5.1        Inquiries are declined only on commercial grounds.

5.2        Commercial grounds are limited to: budget below the operating threshold, scope the
           profile does not deliver, a timeline that cannot be met, or an indication that the work is
           being purchased for resale.

5.3        No other ground is recorded, communicated or applied.

5.4        A decline is approved by the Head of Customer Success and the ground is recorded in
           the inquiry log.

S EC TION 7

Platform Rules

These rules take precedence over any commercial consideration.

7.1        No conversation is moved outside the Fiverr platform before an order exists.

7.2        No payment is taken or proposed outside the Fiverr platform.

7.3        Clients are not asked to provide public reviews or private feedback at any stage.

7.4        Client messages are not answered in kind where the tone is adverse. Adverse exchanges
           are referred to the Head of Customer Success.

7.5        Inquiries are declined only on the commercial grounds set out in Section 5.
