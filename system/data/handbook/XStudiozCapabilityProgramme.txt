S EC TION 1

Objectives

The programme delivers three outcomes. It runs within existing shifts and requires no additional
hours or headcount.
OU TCOME                       P O S I T I O N O N 2 O C T O B ER 2 0 2 6


Capability                     Every team member can confirm a brief, hold a price, price work outside the
                               package, resolve an adverse conversation and lead a client meeting without
                               reference.

Consistency                    The same situation receives the same response irrespective of who is on
                               shift. Matters with a financial consequence continue to be decided by the
                               Head of Customer Success.

Continuity                     Where the Head of Customer Success is unreachable, the team continues
                               to operate within a written delegation.


Capabilities

1.1        Confirm a brief and state the delivery contents within 15 minutes of an order starting.

1.2        Share an initial draft that establishes direction, and obtain substantive feedback rather than
           general approval.

1.3        Collect feedback and assign revisions as a single set.

1.4        Price work falling outside the purchased package rather than absorbing it.

1.5        Resolve an adverse conversation without escalation.

1.6        Lead a client meeting from preparation through to written record.

1.7        Assess whether an order should be refunded in part, continued or concluded, and make a
           recommendation.


   A L I G N M EN T W I T H A S S ES S M EN T

   The capabilities above correspond to the five components Fiverr assesses under effective
   communication, which is the area currently limiting the profile. The programme and the improvement
   in performance are the same work.

Meeting standard

6.1        The full order history is reviewed before the meeting. Three points are prepared: what the
           client has received, what they last requested, and the intended outcome.

6.2        An agenda is stated at the outset and the client is invited to add to it.

6.3        The work is not defended. Dissatisfaction is information.

6.4        No date is committed during the meeting. Availability is confirmed and the date issued
           afterwards.

6.5        A written record is issued within 30 minutes, on the order rather than in conversation alone.

S EC TION 7

Delegated Authority

The Head of Customer Success works seven days a week and holds every decision with a financial
consequence. This section allows the team to continue operating when he is unreachable.

7.1         This delegation is active only when the Head of Customer Success has been unreachable
            for more than two hours.

7.2         Every decision taken under this delegation is recorded with its reason on the same day.

7.3         Every such decision is reviewed within 24 hours of his return, in order to maintain
            consistency.
