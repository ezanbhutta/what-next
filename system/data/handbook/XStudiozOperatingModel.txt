S EC TION 1

Purpose and Scope

This document defines how the XStudioz seller profile is operated. It applies to every member of the
Customer Success team and takes precedence over any earlier operating document.

Scope

1.1        This document covers objectives, the performance areas the profile is scored on, coverage,
           decision authority and the operating rules that apply at all times.

1.2        Order handling is covered separately in OH-01 Order Handbook. Inquiry handling is covered
           in IC-01 Inquiry and Conversion.

1.3        The gig itself is not modified under this document. Packages, pricing, delivery times and
           revision terms remain as published.

1.4        The team does not amend the Fiverr gig or its requirements form.
           A gig carrying 1,565 reviews is returned for review when edited, which places its search position at
           risk. All improvement is achieved through process.

Objectives

3.1        Grow revenue and organic orders.
           No other outcome is treated as an objective. Every measure in this document exists to serve one of
           these two.


3.2        Raise the Success Score from 8 to 9.
           This is the remaining requirement for Top Rated status.


3.3        Convert the recovery in impressions into orders.
           Visibility has improved. Revenue has not. Closing that gap is the priority for August 2026.


3.4        Reduce the value of work held and not moving.
           Currently $10,597 across active orders and unconverted quotes.


Excluded

3.1        Order count in place of order value.
           Higher value orders and more recent orders carry greater weight in the Success Score.


3.2        Queue depth as a target.
           Work in progress is stock, not revenue. It is monitored, never pursued.


3.3        Paid promotion.
           Not used while the Success Score remains below 9.


3.4        Additional gigs.
           The profile continues to operate its existing gigs only.

Performance Areas

Fiverr scores the profile across six areas. Performance is assessed relative to other sellers in the
same category and price range.
A R EA                                                                                                                   A S S ES S M EN T


1. Client satisfaction                                                                                                      Positive

2. Effective communication                                                                                      Room for growth

3. Conflict free orders                                                                                                   Monitored

4. Order cancellations                                                                                                      Positive

5. Delivery time                                                                                                          Monitored

6. Value for money                                                                                                   Strong positive


   EF F EC T I V E C O M M U N I C AT I O N I S T H E S I N G L E A R EA H O L D I N G T H E P R O F I L E B A C K

   This is confirmed by Fiverr on the gig performance panel. Client satisfaction and value for money are
   both assessed positively.


What the area measures
Effective communication is not measured by tone or friendliness. Fiverr assesses five components:

4.1        Availability and speed of response.

4.2        Clarity of the expectations set with the client at the outset.

4.3        Explanation of the process and the stages the client should expect.

4.4        Progress shared during the order rather than only at delivery.

4.5        Issues addressed as they arise rather than at the end.


   U N S EEN F EED B A C K

   Twenty four hours after each order the client completes a private questionnaire covering quality,
   whether the work met expectations and whether it was useful. Responses are not visible to the seller.
   First time buyers and higher value orders carry greater weight. This is why the profile can hold a 4.8
   public rating alongside a Success Score of 8.

S EC TION 5

Coverage and Responsibility

Roles are defined in full in RR-01. This section states how coverage and accountability interact.

5.1        The team member on shift handles all client messages.
           Message handling is not restricted by role. Whoever is on shift responds.

5.2        Accountability for outcomes remains with the role owner at all times.
           A role owner answers for their measures whether or not they were on shift when an event occurred.

5.3        Where two team members are on shift simultaneously, the person on shift acts and the role
           owner decides.

5.4        No shift ends with an unanswered client message.

5.5        No shift ends without a written handover recording actions taken and actions outstanding.

S EC TION 6

Decision Authority

6.1         All matters with a value above USD 500 are decided by the Head of Customer Success.
            This applies to order value, quoted price and any refund or credit.


6.2         All refunds, cancellations and disputes are decided by the Head of Customer Success at
            any value.

6.3         Every decision taken under this section is recorded in writing with the reason.

6.4         Where the Head of Customer Success is unreachable for more than two hours, the
            delegated authority in CP-01 Section 6 applies.

S EC TION 7

Operating Rules

These rules apply at all times and are not subject to shift discretion.

7.1        No shift ends with an unanswered client message.

7.2        No shift ends without a written handover.

7.3        Every shift report records the actions taken during that shift.

7.4        Every material order event is recorded as an internal note.

7.5        No delivery date is committed before designer availability has been confirmed.

7.6        Work outside the purchased package is quoted. It is not absorbed.

7.7        Inquiries are declined only on commercial grounds. Grounds are limited to budget, scope,
           timeline, or an indication that the work is being purchased for resale.

7.8        Refunds, cancellations and disputes are referred to the Head of Customer Success.

7.9        No conversation or payment is moved outside the Fiverr platform.

7.10       Clients are not asked to provide public reviews or private feedback at any stage.
