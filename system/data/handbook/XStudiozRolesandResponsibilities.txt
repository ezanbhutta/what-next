Support


Principles

1.1        The team member on shift handles all client messages. Message handling is not restricted by
           role.

1.2        Accountability for outcomes remains with the role owner whether or not they were on shift.

1.3        Where two team members are on shift simultaneously, the person on shift acts and the role
           owner decides.

1.4        A responsibility that can be performed more reliably by a system is removed from the role.


   VA C A N T P O S I T I O N

   Five roles cover 168 hours a week with no reserve. The period from 01:00 to 09:00 is covered by one
   person for 56 hours a week and produces the highest conversion rate on the profile. The vacant
   position provides a second person during that period.

S EC TION 2

Head of Customer Success


Ezan                                                                         11:00–23:00 Monday to Friday
                                                                          09:00–21:00 Saturday and Sunday
Head of Customer Success

Accountable for the performance of the profile and for every decision with a financial
consequence.

Accountable for

—          The Success Score and the selection of which performance area is addressed

—          All refunds, cancellations and disputes, at any value

—          All matters above USD 500

—          The volume of controlled orders entering the queue

—          Weekly review of each team member

Responsibilities

—          Reviews the daily position and confirms priorities at 11:00

—          Reviews five client conversations each day, selected at random

—          Conducts one review conversation with each team member each week, addressing the two
           measures furthest from standard

—          Works exceptions. Does not review the full order queue

—          Maintains the change log recording every change made, the date and the person responsible

—          Approves quotations and additional work above USD 500

Outside the role

—          Routine client correspondence and inbox clearance

—          Taking over a conversation in place of coaching the team member handling it


    M EA S U R ES

    Success Score · cancellations and disputes · impressions trend · weekly reviews completed

S EC TION 3

Intake and Conversion


Hasnain                                                                        17:00–01:00 Mon–Thu and Sat
                                                                                         Friday 21:00–09:00
Intake and Conversion

Accountable for every inquiry from first contact until an order is placed or the inquiry is closed with
a recorded reason.

Accountable for

—          Conversion of inquiries to orders

—          Three recorded follow ups on every inquiry

—          Recovery of the $5,722 in open quotations

—          Accuracy of every price recorded in the inquiry log

Responsibilities

—          Issues a first response within 10 minutes

—          Completes qualification before any price is provided

—          Records the price in the inquiry log at the time it is sent

—          Follows up on day one, day three and day seven, addressing a different point each time

—          Works open quotations in order of value, contacting each client individually

—          Quotes additional work within the approval threshold

—          Reports spam within 24 hours

Outside the role

—          Quotations above USD 500 without approval

—          Reducing price for unchanged scope

—          Closing an inquiry without three recorded follow ups and a reason

—          Moving a conversation outside the platform


    M EA S U R ES

    First response time · inquiries converted · follow ups recorded · open quotations worked

S EC TION 4

Order Management


Amrah                                                                           09:00–17:00 Mon–Thu and Sat
                                                                                          Friday 07:00–18:00
Order Management

Accountable for every active order from the point it starts until final delivery.

Accountable for

—          Delivery within the committed date

—          Confirmation of the brief on every order

—          The statement of delivery contents given to the client

—          Verification of files before delivery

—          Allocation of work against designer availability

Responsibilities

—          Confirms the brief within 15 minutes of an order starting

—          States the delivery contents to the client before design work begins

—          Issues the confirmed brief to production in ClickUp the same day

—          Shares the initial draft within 48 hours with an explanation of the direction taken

—          Collects all feedback before assigning revisions, and restates the changes before assigning
           them

—          Verifies every file against the stated contents before delivery

—          Works the exception list rather than the full queue

Outside the role

—          Committing a delivery date before confirming designer availability

—          Delivering files that have not been verified

—          Cancelling an order on the platform in order to close it

—          Allowing an order to reach day seven without escalation


    M EA S U R ES

    Delivery within date · briefs confirmed · revision rounds per order · orders reaching day seven

S EC TION 5

Systems and Retention


Zaheen                                                                         09:00–18:00 Monday to Saturday
Systems and Retention

Accountable for the accuracy of the records on which the profile is managed, and for identifying
further work from existing clients.

Accountable for

—          Daily reconciliation of the order tracker against the inquiry log

—          Completeness of the additional work and revenue records

—          The exception list used by Order Management

—          Identification of further work from clients already served

Responsibilities

—          Reconciles the order tracker against the inquiry log each morning and reports any discrepancy
           the same day

—          Records impressions and the seven day average at 09:15

—          Prepares the exception list for the following day at 17:30

—          Maintains the additional work record with no blank entries

—          Maintains the revenue record in the daily ledger

—          Records orders where the client accepted delivery without comment, requested more
           revisions than agreed, or did not respond

—          Identifies further work available from clients already served and refers it to Intake and
           Conversion

Outside the role

—          Requesting public reviews or private feedback from clients

—          Reporting a discrepancy at the end of the week rather than the same day


    M EA S U R ES

    Accuracy of records · discrepancies reported same day · further work identified · repeat orders

S EC TION 6

Night Operations


Nadir                                                                                             21:00–09:00
                                                                                Saturday to Thursday, six nights
Night Operations

Accountable for coverage of the overnight period, which produces the highest conversion rate on
the profile and is covered by one person.

Accountable for

—          First response time throughout the overnight period

—          Completeness of every inquiry handed to Intake and Conversion

—          Identification of adverse client sentiment overnight

—          The 09:00 handover

Responsibilities

—          Issues a first response within 10 minutes at any hour

—          Completes qualification on all four points before handing an inquiry over

—          Records qualification in the inquiry log

—          Answers factual questions on active orders and refers matters requiring a decision

—          Refers adverse client sentiment to the Head of Customer Success immediately, at any hour

—          Reports spam within 24 hours

—          Issues the full handover before 09:00

Outside the role

—          Issuing offers, quoting or negotiating price

—          Declining an inquiry

—          Approving a refund or a cancellation


    M EA S U R ES

    First response time · completeness of qualification · inquiries handed over · quality of handover



    R ES T P ER I O D

    Friday 09:00 to Saturday 21:00. This period is not used for cover, training or delivery.
