# How Fiverr's Automated Systems Read Our Client Chats

**Internal team briefing — CSR, Sales, and Team Leads**
Last verified against Fiverr's official documentation: July 2026

---

## 1. Start here: there is no single "engine"

There is no Fiverr product called a "Success Score and Compliance Engine." That name does not exist in any Fiverr document. If you see it in a blog post or a YouTube video, that source is inventing terminology and you should stop trusting the rest of it.

What actually exists is **two separate systems** that both read the same inbox, for completely different reasons:

| | System A: Success Score | System B: Trust and Safety |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Rank you against competitors | Enforce the rules |
| **Reads** | Order outcomes, buyer feedback, chat quality signals | Message content for policy violations |
| **Output** | A score from 1 to 10 per gig | Warnings, restrictions, suspensions |
| **Speed** | Slow. Weeks and months | Fast. Can hit within hours |
| **Who sees it** | Only us. Buyers see only our Level | Us, by email |
| **Worst case** | Low visibility, no level up | Account gone |

Treat these as two different problems. The habits that protect one do not automatically protect the other.

## 2. Myth check before we go further

Correct information matters more than dramatic information. Here is what is real and what is not.

| Claim you may have heard | Reality |
|---|---|
| "The AI grades your tone and professionalism" | **Partly true.** Fiverr's own community blog states the communication metric looks for negative sentiment and unfriendly or unprofessional tone from either side. But Fiverr has never published how it measures this. |
| "The AI penalises short messages" | **Not documented.** Fiverr says the metric is based on responsiveness, helpfulness, and setting clear expectations. Length is not mentioned anywhere. Long messages do not buy you points. |
| "Deal velocity is tracked, the time from first message to closing a custom order" | **No evidence.** Fiverr publishes exactly one messaging speed metric: response rate, meaning how often you replied to new messages within 24 hours over the last 90 days. No time to close metric exists in any official document. |
| "The system scans 100 percent of chat logs against the Terms of Service" | **Directionally true, unquantified.** Fiverr states it monitors the platform through automated review, human review, or a combination, depending on the case. It has never published a percentage. |
| "Saying the word WhatsApp or email gets you flagged" | **Oversimplified, and this one costs us money.** See section 4. |
| "Fiverr scans for academic dishonesty and banned AI work" | **True.** Both have dedicated policies. Voice cloning of real people and deepfakes are explicitly prohibited. |

## 3. System A: the Success Score

### What it actually measures

Fiverr evaluates each **gig** across six areas, then combines gig scores into your overall score:

1. **Client satisfaction** — reviews and other happiness signals
2. **Effective communication** — responsiveness, helpfulness, ability to set clear expectations
3. **Conflict free orders** — how often disputes and issues arise
4. **Order cancellations** — the effect of cancellations on the order process
5. **Delivery time** — how often you deliver on time or early
6. **Value for money** — whether the client feels they got what they paid for

Both **numerical data** and **descriptive data** feed each area. Descriptive data includes written feedback. This is the closest Fiverr comes to admitting language analysis, and it stops there.

### Four facts most sellers get wrong

**It is relative, not absolute.** Your score is benchmarked against other freelancers **in your price range and subcategory**. A 4.9 rating means nothing if every competitor at your price point is also at 4.9 and beating you on private feedback. In logo design, one of the most saturated subcategories on the platform, the bar is set by very strong operators.

**Private feedback is the hidden variable.** Buyers can leave private feedback for up to **60 days** after an order. It never appears on your profile. It does affect your score. This is why a gig can show all 5 star public reviews and still carry a Success Score of 4. We cannot see the leak.

**It moves slowly by design.** Fiverr states the score analyses patterns over time and one or two bad orders will not crash it overnight. The flip side is that recovery is equally slow. There is no quick fix.

**Weighting is uneven.** Gigs with more orders carry more weight. Higher value and more recent orders may carry more weight within a gig. Our high ticket orders matter disproportionately.

### What does NOT hurt the score

Fiverr states these explicitly:

- **Unresponsive clients.** If the buyer goes silent, the system accounts for it.
- **Revisions, delivery extensions, and partial refunds.** These are judged only by their effect on overall client satisfaction. They are treated as tools to fix an experience, not as failures.
- **Inactivity.** The score does not drop just because orders slowed down.

**Operational takeaway:** stop being afraid of extensions and partial refunds. Being afraid of them is what pushes CSRs into arguments, and arguments are what actually damage the conflict free and communication metrics.

## 4. System B: Trust and Safety, and the off platform rule we get wrong

This is where accounts die. Read this section twice.

### The rule everyone misquotes

Fiverr's actual policy text:

> Communication through third party tools is permitted **only after an order has been placed.**

And on contact details, the prohibition is on sharing email addresses, phone numbers, or third party messaging apps **with the sole purpose of conducting business off platform.**

So the real rule is not "never type the word email." The real rule is:

- **Before an order exists:** everything stays in the Fiverr inbox. No exceptions. This is the dangerous window.
- **After an order is placed:** third party tools are allowed, but Fiverr instructs you to document the conversation and post notes or summaries back on the order page.
- **Payment:** never off platform. There is no window where this is acceptable. Ever.

### What triggers enforcement

- Offering or accepting payment outside Fiverr
- Proposing to bypass Fiverr's payment system
- Directing a client to an external platform or external contract
- Sharing contact details for the purpose of doing business off platform
- Multiple accounts operated by one person
- Pressuring a client for a review or a higher rating
- Academic and workplace dishonesty
- Prohibited AI work: cloned voices of real people, deepfakes, likenesses used without permission

### The enforcement ladder

| Stage | What happens |
|---|---|
| Warning | Sent to the account email. **Expires after 90 days.** Does not limit activity, but can freeze your level progression |
| Second warning, same violation | Suspension |
| Three warnings, different violations | Suspension |
| Account restriction | 60 days of limited access to finish existing orders, then permanent suspension |
| Suspension | No login. Funds held 90 days |
| Appeal | Must be filed within 6 months |

Two gig removals within 60 days can themselves trigger an account warning.

## 5. The rules for our team

**Rule 1. Answer every new inquiry inside 24 hours.** This is the only messaging speed metric Fiverr actually publishes. Level 1 needs 80 percent, Level 2 and Top Rated need 90 percent over a rolling 90 day window. Archive what you will not answer. Never ignore.

**Rule 2. Never move a pre order conversation off Fiverr.** Not to email, not to WhatsApp, not to a call link. If the buyer pushes, use the approved reply in section 6.

**Rule 3. Set the scope in writing, in the chat, before work starts.** Number of concepts, number of revisions, file formats, delivery date. Put it in the order page thread where the system can read it. Verbal or off platform agreements do not exist as far as Fiverr is concerned.

**Rule 4. Never argue.** Not once. Not even when the buyer is wrong, rude, or manipulating. The communication and conflict free metrics read negative sentiment **from either side**. A buyer being aggressive is not what hurts us. Us matching their energy is. Stay flat, stay warm, offer a solution.

**Rule 5. Deliver only finished work through the Delivery button.** Fiverr states drafts go in the order message thread, not through delivery. Fake early deliveries to protect the on time metric are visible and they damage client satisfaction instead.

**Rule 6. Chase the silent buyer within 24 hours of delivery.** Orders auto complete after 3 days, and private feedback stays open for 60 days after that. A silent buyer who never spoke to us is a private feedback risk we cannot see.

**Rule 7. Never ask for a review or a rating.** Pressuring a buyer for feedback is an Integrity and Authenticity violation, and it is one of the fastest ways to a warning.

## 6. Approved replies when a buyer wants to go off platform

Use these as written. Warm, plain English, no dashes.

**When they ask for WhatsApp or email before an order:**

> Happy to talk this through properly. I keep everything here on Fiverr so we both have a full record of what we agreed, and so the platform can back us up if anything ever needs sorting. Once your order is placed we can also jump on a video call right here. What is the deadline you are working toward?

**When they ask to pay outside Fiverr:**

> I understand wanting to keep it simple, but paying outside Fiverr removes the protection for both of us and it is against the platform rules, which could cost us both our accounts. Let me put together a custom offer here instead so you are fully covered. What is your budget range?

**When they push a second time:**

> I really do want to work with you on this. Fiverr is where my whole business lives, so I cannot take work outside it. Everything we need is available right here, including calls and file sharing. Shall I send the offer over?

## 7. What we honestly do not know

Do not let anyone on the team pretend otherwise.

- Fiverr has **never published** the algorithm, the weights, or the thresholds for any of the six areas.
- We do not know whether the language model reads full conversations, samples them, or only reads flagged ones.
- We cannot see our own private feedback, so we cannot audit what is dragging a gig down.
- Third party guides, including the widely shared 2026 ones, contain figures that directly contradict Fiverr's official help centre. Treat every non Fiverr source as unverified.

**The team rule on sources:** if a claim is not in `help.fiverr.com` or on `fiverr.com/community/standards`, it is a rumour. Do not build a process on a rumour.

## 8. What this means for how we run the agency

**The score is per gig and per profile.** Storm, Dygram, Grid, Eikon, and XStudioz each carry their own scores against their own price bands. A CSR habit that damages one profile does not damage the others, but it also means a fix on one does not lift the rest. Score health has to be tracked per profile, not as one agency number.

**Price band is the benchmark, so raising prices changes the competition.** Moving a gig up a price tier moves us into a different peer group. That cuts both ways and should be a deliberate decision, not a side effect.

**Private feedback is invisible, so we need a proxy.** Since we cannot see the damage, we should build our own signal: log every order where the buyer went silent, asked for more than the agreed revisions, or accepted delivery without a word. Those are the orders most likely leaking private feedback. Track them in the CSR sheet and review the pattern monthly.

**The real enemy is the argument, not the algorithm.** Every metric in this document degrades through the same mechanism, which is friction in the chat. Scope clarity up front removes most of it. That is a training problem and a template problem, not a technology problem.
