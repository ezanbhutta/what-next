The Algorithm Model
How Fiverr actually ranks in 2026, veriﬁed against platform documentation.
2.1  What Fiverr states directly
Search results only show freelancers currently available to take orders.
Ranking combines textual relevance with popularity and buyer-satisfaction signals, driven by machine-learning models, review
scores, pricing, availability and responsiveness.
Fiverr prioritises fast deliverers when it detects buyer urgency.
Pricing is itself a ranking input — Fiverr deliberately mixes price points to give buyers a comparison range.
Positions rotate continuously. There is no ﬁxed rank to hold.
The Fiverr's Choice badge is assigned, not applied for, and rotates. It is a downstream eﬀect of quality signals, never a target.

2.3  The three mechanics most sellers miss
A The score is peer-relative, not absolute
Fiverr benchmarks you against other freelancers in your subcategory and your price range. You can improve in absolute
terms and still fall, if the ﬁeld improves faster. Logo design is one of the most saturated subcategories on the platform —
which is precisely why Fiverr's own success managers recommend opening gigs in thinner subcategories to lift scores across
the board.
B Weighting is not even
Gigs with more orders carry more weight in the overall score. Within a gig, higher-value and more recent orders hit harder.
A $400 branding kit moves your score substantially more than a $45 order.
XStudioz Operating Model · HaseebMadeIt · 27 July 2026 9
C Private ratings are invisible and heavily weighted
Twenty-four hours after the public review, buyers get an optional anonymous questionnaire: overall quality of the delivery,
how closely it met expectations, whether it was useful. It can be submitted up to 60 days after completion. First-time
buyers' private ratings are weighted more heavily than repeat customers', and higher-priced, higher-volume orders carry
more weight. This is why a wall of 5-star public reviews can sit alongside a falling Success Score.
THE STRATEGIC IMPLICATION
Order volume is no longer a ranking currency. The system rewards new buyers, at higher prices, delivered fast, with no
disputes and no cancellations — and it measures satisfaction through a channel you cannot see. Any strategy that
maximises transaction count while diluting per-order value and new-buyer satisfaction is pushing against the highest-
weighted signals in the model.

2.7  Why the old playbook stopped working
Fiverr's trajectory is a steady migration from gameable volume signals to unobservable satisfaction signals. Early Fiverr ranked
largely on conversion rate and order volume. The 2024 overhaul replaced monthly evaluation with daily evaluation, introduced the
Success Score, added a 30-day grace period, made scoring peer-relative, and moved the heaviest weight onto private feedback that
sellers never see. The 2025–26 layer added AI-assisted matching and brief routing gated on Success Score.
The practical consequence: tactics built on producing transaction volume have declining and eventually negative returns, because
they dilute the exact signals that now carry the most weight — new-buyer satisfaction, order value, and dispute-free delivery.
