# Playbook — Stalled and dead orders

## The 30-day line

Under 30 days silent, an order is **stalled** — worth one well-crafted nudge.
Over 30 days silent, it is **finished** — close the record.

Your tracker currently has orders 137 and 148 days silent still marked On Hold. Those are
not stalled; they are inflating the pipeline and hiding your real active count. Chasing
them costs goodwill and returns nothing.

## Reviving a genuinely stalled order

One message. Make saying yes require no thought:

> "Hi — just circling back on the [specific thing you sent]. Do you want me to proceed
> with that direction, or would you rather I explore something different? Either is fine,
> just let me know which."

One closed question. No apology, no pressure, no third option.

- **No reply in 48h** → move to On Hold and stop.
- **Never send a third follow-up.** F3 has converted 0 of 19 in your data.

## Closing a dead order

Send one final message that leaves the door open and asks for nothing:

> "Hi — I'll close this one out for now so it's not sitting open on your side. Everything
> we made is yours and I've kept the files. If you pick the project back up, just message
> me and I'll jump straight back in."

Then mark it **Dead** in the tracker the same day.

Buyers who are closed out cleanly come back warm. Buyers who are chased for five months
do not come back at all.
