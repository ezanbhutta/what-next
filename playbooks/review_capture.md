# Playbook — Review capture

**Current state:** 13.6% of the 597 orders on tabs that track reviews have one recorded.
At the present order rate, lifting capture toward 65% is worth roughly **+139 reviews
over 90 days**.

Review velocity is a direct gig-ranking input. It is also the only growth lever available
here that costs nothing and carries zero platform risk — unlike VVRO, which is currently
capped precisely because it carries risk.

## Make it automatic, not remembered

Add the request to the **delivery message template**. Anything that depends on a CSR
remembering at the end of a long order will run at roughly the rate you see today.

## Ask at approval, not after delivery

The peak of goodwill is the moment they say "yes, that's it" — not three days later when
the files are in a folder and the excitement has faded.

## The wording

> "Delighted this one landed. If you have a moment, a short review helps enormously —
> it's genuinely the main way new clients find us. And if anything at all is missing from
> the package, tell me first and I'll fix it before you write anything."

That last sentence matters. It routes dissatisfaction to you privately instead of into a
public 3-star.

## Never

- **Never ask for 5 stars, or for a specific rating.** It violates Fiverr ToS and puts
  the account at risk. The account is worth more than every order in the pipeline.
- Never offer anything in exchange for a review.

## Record it

Fill the REVIEW column every time. **"no review" is data; blank is not.** The engine
cannot distinguish "asked and declined" from "never asked" while the column is blank, and
those two need completely different fixes.
