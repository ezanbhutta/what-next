# Playbook — Upsell

**Measured signal:** inquiries where an upsell was attempted converted **54.3% (n=70)**
against **30.8% (n=929)** without. z = 4.05, so the gap is not noise.

**The honest caveat:** this is observational. CSRs choose who to upsell, and they choose
warm leads. Some of that 76% relative lift is selection, not causation. The engine will
not let you bank the full number until the A/B test below has run.

## The A/B test (run this first)

For the next 100 inbound inquiries, alternate strictly by arrival order: odd-numbered
leads get an upsell attempt, even-numbered do not. **CSRs must not choose** — that choice
is exactly the bias being measured. Log the arm in the Upsell column. The engine reports
the de-biased effect at n≥100 per arm.

## Where the money actually is

Your AOV is **$137** with a **median of $100** and a Q3 of **$175**. The distribution is
bottom-heavy: most orders sit in the cheapest tier. Every $1 of AOV is worth more than
every extra order right now, because the organic constraint caps volume but nothing caps
price.

## The three windows

**1. At inquiry, before the order.** The buyer has budget in mind but has not committed.
Anchor at the package above the one they asked about — describe what they get, not what
it costs. Then let them choose down.

**2. At approval, before delivery.** Highest-yield moment and the most under-used. They
have just seen work they love. This is when brand guidelines, a social kit or stationery
sells itself.

> "Really glad this landed. Since the mark is locked, the natural next step is the
> brand guidelines document — it's what keeps the logo being used correctly once your
> team and printers start touching it. Want me to add it?"

**3. After delivery, on a warm exit.** Buyers who left happy and went quiet. `andrem530`
in your tracker is the archetype: full brand identity, went well, mentioned future
projects, then silence. That is a warm lead, not a dead one.

> "Your PEAK21 identity has been out in the world a few months now. If you're rolling it
> onto anything new — deck templates, packaging, a sub-brand — happy to keep it consistent."

## Rules

- Lead with what they already own, never with a price.
- Anchor in the **$151–$260** band. It is your Q3 and it lands with buyers who trust you.
- **Log every attempt**, TRUE or FALSE. The column is currently 0.0% filled across 492
  orders, which is why this lever cannot be measured or defended.
- One offer per message. Two offers is a menu, and menus get postponed.
