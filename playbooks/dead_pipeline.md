## The message

> Hi [name], following up on the [service] we discussed on [date].
> The quote we shared was [price] and that is still open.
> If the timing was wrong, tell me when to check back.
> If the budget was the issue, tell me your number and I will see what I can put together.

Two questions, both easy to answer, neither requiring them to re-open the whole brief.

## The follow-up engine

No inquiry may be closed as "Not Placed" until FollowUp 1, 2 and 3 carry dates.
A CSR who marks a lead dead without three logged touches has it reopened and
reassigned.

| Touch | When | Angle |
|---|---|---|
| Follow-up 1 | Day 1 | Logged with a date |
| Follow-up 2 | Day 3 | **Different angle** — address the likely objection, not a repeat of the offer |
| Follow-up 3 | Day 7 | Final touch. Only now may the lead be closed, with a reason code |
