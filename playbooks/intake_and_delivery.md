# Playbook — Intake, checkpoint, and delivery

The operating model's twelve-step lifecycle, in the order it happens. Two of the
six scored Success Score areas — on-time delivery and value for money — are
decided here, and both are currently dragging at 4.8.

## Service levels

| Checkpoint | Standard | Escalate |
|---|---|---|
| First response to inquiry | 30 minutes | Over 2 hours → shift lead |
| Intake posted after order start | 15 minutes | Over 1 hour → shift lead |
| First concepts to buyer | 48 hours | Over 72 hours → Ash |
| Mid-order checkpoint | 50% elapsed | Missed → logged as a defect |
| Final delivery | 60% of promised window | Over 100% → Ash, same day |
| Any order age | 7 days | Hard escalation to Ash |
| Any cancellation risk | — | Ash **before** it is filed, always |

Observed cycle time is 4–12 days against a 2–3 day category median. Fiverr
prioritises fast deliverers when it detects buyer urgency, so this is not just a
satisfaction issue — it is a distribution issue.

## The 12-question intake

Gig requirements cannot currently be edited, so this runs **in order chat within
15 minutes of order start**, as one numbered block. Ask the client to reply inline.

**Brand basics**
1. Exact brand name as it should appear, including capitalisation. Any tagline?
2. What does your business actually do, in one or two sentences?
3. Who is your customer? Age, market, price level.

**Direction**
4. Send 3 logos you like — any industry — and one line on what you like about each.
5. Send 1 logo you actively dislike, and why.
6. Icon plus text, text only, or open to both?
7. Any colours that must be used, or must be avoided?

**Constraints**
8. Where will this be used most? Website, packaging, signage, app icon, print?
9. Anything that must appear in the mark — a specific object, letter or symbol?
10. Is there an existing logo we are replacing? Send it.

**Scope lock — the one that protects the score**
11. Confirming your package includes: *[list every file and asset by name]*. Is
    anything you expected missing from that list?
12. Your delivery date is *[date]*. Does that work, or do you need it sooner?

### Why question 11 exists

One visible 4.7 review cites **missing files and a watermark left on a social media
cover**. A sheet annotation records a **cancellation caused by a client who did not
know an editable file needs the font installed locally**. Both failures hit quality
of delivery and value of delivery simultaneously — the exact two sub-ratings
holding the profile at 4.8.

Confirming the deliverable list in writing before work starts kills the complaint
and hands the QA gate a checklist.

## The QA gate

Owned by the **Operations Lead, not the designer**. Nothing ships without a check
against the question-11 list:

- Every named file present, in every named format.
- Watermarks removed. Check the social covers specifically.
- Fonts noted, with a line telling the client they must install them for editable files.
- Vector source files included.

## Delivery

Deliver at **60% of the promised window**. If the gig says 5 days, deliver on day 3.

**Only finished work goes through the Delivery button.** Drafts go in the order
message thread. Fake early deliveries to protect the on-time metric are visible to
Fiverr and damage client satisfaction instead.
