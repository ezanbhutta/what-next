# Playbook — Lead triage

Not all inquiries deserve equal effort. Spending the same CSR minutes on every lead is
the largest single misallocation visible in the data.

## Conversion by country (n = 1,000 inquiries)

| Tier | Countries | Conversion |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Netherlands 61.9%, Australia 49.0%, Italy 41.2%, United States 39.2%, France 35.0% | high |
| **2** | United Kingdom 33.3%, Canada 27.5%, Germany 22.2% | medium |
| **3** | United Arab Emirates 11.5%, India 3.8% | low |

Ranked on the Wilson lower bound in the daily brief, so a small sample cannot outrank a
large one.

## Effort allocation

- **Tier 1** — full custom quote, offer a call, personalised concept notes.
- **Tier 2** — standard reply, one follow-up maximum.
- **Tier 3** — template reply, no follow-up, never a meeting slot.

Tier 3 leads are not worthless, but they should cost minutes, not hours.

## Follow-ups: stop at two

| Depth | n | Placed | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| No follow-up | 640 | 225 | 35.2% |
| F1 only | 305 | 94 | 30.8% |
| F1+F2 | 36 | 5 | 13.9% |
| F1+F2+F3 | 19 | 0 | **0.0%** |

**Read this carefully.** The descending pattern is mostly survivorship bias — only leads
that have already gone cold get chased, so follow-ups look like they cause failure when
they are really a symptom of it. **This does not prove follow-ups hurt.**

What it does prove is narrower and still actionable: **F3 has converted 0 of 19.** Whatever
the causal story, those CSR hours have returned nothing. Cap the ladder at two touches and
move the freed time to upsells on existing buyers, where the measured gap is real.

## Shift

Morning converts **38.5%** (n=650). Evening converts **22.0%** (n=127). Night converts
26.3% (n=95). Before reassigning staff, check whether Evening's gap is lead *quality* or
lead *handling* — compare the country mix between shifts. If Evening leads are structurally
worse, cut Evening staffing rather than trying to coach conversion upward.

## Scammers

10 leads are noted as scammers. Pattern: urgent, vague brief, pushes to move off-platform,
asks for work before order. Never deliver anything before an order is placed, and never
move to WhatsApp/email before payment.
